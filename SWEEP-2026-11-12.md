## Fixed

1. **Page footers said May, the dataset said October.** All 12 entity pages
   × 5 locales carried a hand-written "Last verified 2026-05-01 / 05-20" while
   `entities.json` said 2026-10-01. New `build_stamps.py` writes the dataset
   date into the footer; guard invariant 31 blocks the deploy on drift.
2. **`entities.json` claimed to be generated 2026-05-20** while containing
   October dates. `build_stamps.py` now refreshes `generated` whenever the data
   has moved past it (not on every run); same invariant checks it.
3. **Trial date (open item 1 since August) — resolved as proposed.** The MoT
   release is dated Saturday 15 Nov 2025 and says "the trial operation today";
   EHang's release is dated 17 Nov and gives no flight date. Every sentence
   that dated the *flight* to 17 Nov now says 15 Nov (356 mentions, 5 locales,
   JSON-LD and meta included); every reference to EHang's 17 Nov *release* is
   untouched. `mot-qatar` no longer says "ahead of the public 17 November
   flights" — no source describes separate public flights. Timeline entries
   that dated the flight to the release day were redated and now cite the MoT
   release, which was added to the source list of the four pages that lacked it
   (`doha-evtol-vs-taxi-metro`, `gulf-evtol-comparison-uae-vs-qatar`,
   `qatar-autonomous-evtol-explained`, `qatar-evtol-vision-2030`).
4. **Metro time.** `doha-evtol-vs-taxi-metro`'s description said the metro
   takes ~30 min; its own fact grid and body say ~15. Description, hub cards
   (5 locales), feed, CSV, API and llms files now say ~15.
5. Guard invariant 32: every in-page `#anchor` must resolve (found on the UAE
   site; this repo was clean).
